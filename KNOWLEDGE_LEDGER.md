# Knowledge Ledger

The ledger is the audit trail. Every cycle records what was asked, what was observed, what was concluded, what remains uncertain, and what changed.

Required fields for each decision:

```text
Question:
Decision:
Knowledge labels:
Evidence and provenance:
Disconfirming evidence sought:
Confidence:
Artifacts changed:
Review trigger:
Next action:
```

Rejected hypotheses and deprecated skills are retained, not silently deleted.
