# Quality Rubric

Score each dimension 0–3 and record evidence:

| Dimension | 0 | 3 |
|---|---|---|
| Correctness | unsupported or wrong | independently supported and tested |
| Scope | ambiguous or overbroad | precise triggers and limits |
| Actionability | descriptive only | executable procedure and examples |
| Robustness | no edge cases | counterexamples and recovery paths |
| Evidence | circular or absent | primary, independent, current sources |
| Maintainability | no owner or review trigger | provenance and explicit refresh conditions |
| Safety | unexamined harms | misuse and risk controls |

A skill cannot become `trusted` with any zero, or without recorded validation evidence and adversarial review.
